# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge to create an environment of respect, inclusion, and collaboration where everyone feels safe to participate, contribute, and grow.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful:** Listen to differing perspectives with an open mind
- **Being inclusive:** Welcome contributors from all backgrounds and experience levels
- **Being constructive:** Provide helpful feedback and suggestions for improvement
- **Being collaborative:** Work together to solve problems and achieve goals
- **Being honest:** Communicate clearly and transparently
- **Being professional:** Keep discussions focused on the project and community
- **Giving credit:** Acknowledge and appreciate others' contributions

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
- Offensive comments or language
- Deliberate attacks or disrespect
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Trolling or spam
- Any form of abuse or bullying
- Sexual harassment or inappropriate content

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, PRs)
- Official project events
- Community interactions related to the project
- Representation of the project in public spaces

## Enforcement

### Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document** the incident (when, where, what happened)
2. **Report** to the project maintainers via email or private message
3. **Provide** relevant details and context
4. **Allow time** for investigation

Reports will be handled confidentially and professionally.

### Consequences

Community members who violate the Code of Conduct will face consequences proportional to the severity:

1. **Warning:** First-time minor violations
2. **Temporary Suspension:** Repeated violations or serious misconduct
3. **Permanent Ban:** Severe or repeated violations

Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, issues, PRs, or ban users at their discretion.

## Reporting Guidelines

**Report To:**
- Project Maintainers (private communication preferred)
- GitHub's community guidelines reporting
- Email: [maintainer email if available]

**Include:**
- Description of what happened
- When and where it occurred
- Who was involved
- Any relevant links or screenshots
- Your contact information (optional)

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:
- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate action in response to violations
- Protecting the privacy of reporters
- Maintaining a welcoming community
- Setting a positive example
